# Meshron

A private messenger with nobody in the middle. Messages and calls go straight from your phone to your friend's. No server, no phone number, no account.

- **Works without internet:** same Wi-Fi or hotspot, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct, and mesh hand-over through friends' phones.
- **Private:** end-to-end encrypted messages, voice calls and video calls. No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
- **Easy:** find friends by @username or QR code, group chats, photos and files, recovery key backup.

## Download

Get the latest APK from **[Releases](https://github.com/xalvxn-dev/meshron/releases/latest)**.

- Most phones: `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk`
- Older 32-bit phones: `app-armeabi-v7a-release.apk`

Android 10 or newer. Your phone will ask you to allow installs from your browser or file manager.
