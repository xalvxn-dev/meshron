## Full description (max 4000)
Meshron is a messenger with nobody in the middle. Your messages and calls go straight from your phone to your friend's. There is no server storing your chats, no phone number and no account.

WORKS WITHOUT INTERNET
• Same Wi-Fi or hotspot: chat and call even when the network has no internet.
• Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct: phones close by connect directly, no router needed.
• Mesh: friends' phones can carry your locked messages and hand them over when they meet the person. They can't read them.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• End-to-end encrypted messages, voice calls and video calls.
• No phone number, no email, no account. Just pick a name.
• Your key, chats and contacts are encrypted on your phone.
• Compare safety codes to make sure you're talking to the right person.
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

EASY TO USE
• Find friends by @username, scan their QR code, or share your ID.
• Voice and video calls over the internet, Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.
• Photos and files, group chats, delivery ticks.
• Back up your account with a recovery key.
• Block anyone, delete messages or whole chats any time.

Good to know: messages reach people whenever your phones can connect. Over the internet that's instant. Without internet it happens when you're near each other, or when someone carries the message. Calls need a live connection.
